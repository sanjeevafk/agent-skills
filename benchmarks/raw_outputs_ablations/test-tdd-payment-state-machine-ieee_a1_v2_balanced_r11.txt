### Design Strengths

- **State enum is str-based** — clean serialization everywhere
- **RLock protects all mutations** — thread-safe without reentrant issues
- **History returns shallow copy** — immutable from caller perspective (`list(self._history)`)
- **Data-driven transitions** — rules mapped via frozenset dicts, not nested if/elif
- **Clean domain event** — frozen `TransitionEvent` dataclass with extensible `extra` dict
- **Callback support** — `on_transition`/`remove_transition_handler` with safe iteration
